Methodology

System projection: The "system total at end date" is auto-projected from Solstice's live published total plus the 7-day average daily emission rate. Changing the end date recalculates this in real time.

SLX allocation: Solstice has not announced the Season 3 allocation (the campaign publishes a 7.5%-of-supply base across all seasons). The 3% default is what S2 settled at — a familiar anchor, not a forecast. Outputs scale linearly with it.

Season 3: Runs 2026-08-01 → 2026-12-01. Main quests: Hold USX 10×, Hold eUSX 2×, Hold JR strcUSX 20×, Hold SR strcUSX 15×, Exponent Dec26 YT (USX 32×, eUSX 15×) and LP (USX 22×, eUSX 10×), Orca/Raydium USX pools up to 20×, Kamino kVault 20×.

Loyalty multiplier: Applied once at S3 end based on retained TVL vs your S2 position: ≥100% → 1.4×, 50–99% → 1.3×, below 50% → removed. Newcomers get their own boost instead. If your 1.4× exceeds the network average (e.g. 1.2×), your effective share grows by ≈1.4÷1.2 = 17%. If everyone holds the same multiplier, it cancels out in the ratio.
