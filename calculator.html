Methodology

System projection: The "system total at end date" is auto-projected from Solstice's live published total plus the 7-day average daily emission rate. Changing the end date recalculates this in real time.

SLX allocation: Current docs anchor is 3% of total supply (30M SLX). Rumored bumps to 3.25%–3.5% if Dynamic Distribution activates based on TVL thresholds.

Loyalty multiplier: Applied once at S2 end based on retained TVL vs your S1 TWA baseline. If your 1.4× exceeds the network average (e.g. 1.2×), your effective share grows by ≈1.4÷1.2 = 17%. If everyone holds the same multiplier, it cancels out in the ratio.
