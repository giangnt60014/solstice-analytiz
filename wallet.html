Methodology

Balance estimates: When no Helius RPC key is configured, token balances are back-calculated as daily_emission ÷ multiplier. Configure a free Helius key in the Calculator sidebar to enable direct on-chain reads.

Projections: YT positions are capped at Exponent maturity (Jun 2026 / Sep 2026). Non-YT positions (HOLD, LP) earn linearly until season end. System total projected from Solstice live API + 7-day average daily rate.

LP / lend / borrow: Protocol-internal positions (Kamino lending, Loopscale borrow, Exponent YT via event-walked accounts) may not appear if not indexed by Hanyon. Totals sourced from the analytics index are authoritative.
