Methodology

Vault balance: Reads the USDC token account HwdwwKH…EQL owned by the Bulktrade vault — this is the live total USDC locked.

User deposit: Scans your wallet's transaction history since June 1, 2026. Any transaction that interacts with the Bulktrade vault is parsed to find the net USDC change in the vault's token account — positive = deposit, negative = withdrawal. Net result = your current effective deposit.

Share calculation: your deposit ÷ total vault USDC at the time of each Saturday distribution. This page uses the current vault balance as a proxy for past distribution snapshots.

Weekly AURA: 1,000,000 AURA every Saturday at 00:00 UTC. Your allocation = share × 1,000,000. Past distributions (before loading this page) are estimated using your current share as a constant approximation.
